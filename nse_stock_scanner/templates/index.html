How the score works

Return estimate: HistGradientBoostingRegressor predicts the next 20-trading-day adjusted-close return using trailing returns, moving-average gaps, volatility, RSI, volume trend, and drawdown.

Walk-forward evaluation: Historical forecast origins are tested in time order. Training rows are purged unless their 20-day target would already have been known at the forecast date. This is intended to reduce look-ahead leakage, but the test sample is small and overlapping market regimes remain a limitation.

Risk score: A 0–100 heuristic based on recent annualized volatility and trailing drawdown. It is not a standardized or personalized risk measure.

Important limitations: The scanner does not include company fundamentals, corporate announcements, macroeconomic conditions, taxes, slippage, liquidity impact, or a full portfolio risk model. The 0.30% round-trip cost is only an editable assumption; real costs vary by broker, trade size, taxes, and market conditions. Validate with more history and a paper-trading period before considering real trades.
Market data is retrieved using yfinance from Yahoo Finance and may be delayed, incomplete, rate-limited, or revised. See README.md for setup and limitations.
